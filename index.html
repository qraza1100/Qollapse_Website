Our first title

The Last Observer

The Bureau of Determined Outcomes once kept reality in order, one observation at a time. Six weeks ago it suffered a coherence failure. Nothing exploded; the building simply stopped being decided. Its staff are unresolved, and whatever is looked at becomes final.

You are the last Field Observer, armed with the Calibration Gauntlet.

Signature chapter

A Nobel-recognised experiment, turned into a puzzle

In the Correspondence Office, you and Vira sit in sealed booths, answer separate questions and can never communicate. Ordinary strategies win at most 75% of rounds. Sharing an entangled quantum state lets you reach about 85%.

This is the Bell (CHSH) test, the effect shown by the experiments recognised with the 2022 Nobel Prize in Physics.

84%

of audits (16 wins in 20 rounds) pass with the quantum strategy

41%

pass by luck with the best ordinary strategy

Checked on our engine: 1,500 audits played through it passed 83.3% of the time (theory 84.2%); the ordinary strategy passed 41.1% (theory 41.5%).

Three endings

The final observation decides how it ends

Observe

Settle everything

Vira's fate is decided, for better or worse. The facility recovers and you walk out into daylight.

Contain

Take her place

The measurement turns on you. Vira walks free; the building keeps the Observer.

Refuse

Choose nothing

Walk away unmeasured. Nothing is learned, and the loop begins again.

The chosen ending is remembered: the next playthrough's announcements, terminals and dialogue change. There is no "correct" answer and no pressure.

The Qollapse Engine

Real physics, computed live by our own engine

Built in-house in C++ with no third-party dependencies. It runs offline on the player's PC: no account, no internet, no personal data. A chamber's physics takes about a microsecond per frame.
